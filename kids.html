Story Playground

Make a tiny picture book in five easy steps.

1. Pick your hero

A hero is the person, animal, or thing we follow.

Learn: Be specific.Clear details help us picture your idea.

2. What do they want?

Then add one problem that gets in the way.

Learn: Emotional core.The feeling that makes us care.

3. Draw four pictures

Use your finger or mouse. Add one short sentence under each picture.

Learn: Story spine.Beginning, problem, try, and ending.

4. Make one picture move

Pick one simple camera move for the biggest moment.

Learn: Tracking shot.The camera follows the action.
