Input Idea
A warm, playful animated short about an old man who plays alone.
Genre: Comedy DramaMood: Warm · Playful 3D · American Animationgrounded in «Geri's Game» (Pixar)
Core Twist

The underdog—the timid self—wins through deception rather than force. Because the opponent is also himself, every victory is simultaneously a defeat: the irony of beating oneself at one's own game. (Setup: beat 2 reveals the self-match → payoff: beat 4 weaponizes the feint.)

Mise-en-scène Motif

Opposing chairs symbolize a divided self. The empty autumn park encodes solitude and life's twilight. A low-angle chess insert enlarges an internal threat; warm amber carries nostalgia, while the humble denture prize restores humor.
