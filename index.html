Learn to Direct with AI · For People Who Never Studied Film

Never studied film?
Direct one anyway — and learn how.

E.V.E Creator is a shared learning platform for anyone curious about AI film and AI art. Bring a mood, genre, or scene, and an AI film crew designs the story, structure, storyboard, and shot-level prompts — teaching you why every choice works, grounded in frame-level analysis of master animation. Tap any craft term to learn it. Then take the plan and make the film yourself. You keep authorship; E.V.E gives you the crew and the film education.

Why E.V.E is different
The Creator's Room

Other tools hand you one draft. E.V.E gives you a film crew.

A single idea becomes a blueprint — then a five-role AI crew (Director · Writer · Cinematographer · Editor · Test Audience) critiques it against analyzed film craft, applies concrete fixes, and re-scores it. You watch the film measurably improve — and every note teaches a real film principle you can tap to learn. Powered by GPT-5.6, built with Codex.
