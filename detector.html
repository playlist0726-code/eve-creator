What this can—and cannot—see

A transparent preflight, not an AI critic.

  • 01Can: sample pixel brightness, contrast, saturation, edge detail, frame change, and metadata.
  • 02Can: decode audio locally when the browser supports the codec and the file is not too large.
  • 03Cannot: judge whether your story, acting, composition, or style is good.
  • 04Cannot: reliably detect every cut from sparse samples or diagnose codec/color-management issues.
Thresholds are learning heuristics. A dark, quiet, static, or monochrome shot may be exactly the right creative decision.
