# First Frame — Opening Title Sequence
**Runtime:** ~14s · **Cuts:** 5 · **Aspect:** 16:9 master + 1:1 crop · **Audio:** music only, no VO

---

## The idea

The product is called **First Frame** because the hardest moment in filmmaking is the
*empty* first frame. So the opening dramatizes exactly that:

> A small creator stands before a blank frame and doesn't know what to do.
> One spark. The cape appears. One becomes five — a crew.
> The crew fills the frame. The frames become a film.
> The film resolves into the name.

**Empty → spark → crew → film → brand.** Every cut is *caused* by the one before it.
That is what makes it a story instead of a montage.

## The through-line (this is what makes the morph feel designed)

**One rectangle carries the entire sequence.** It is:

| Cut | What the rectangle is |
|---|---|
| 1 | The blank frame — a wall of intimidating white |
| 2 | A canvas accepting its first mark |
| 3 | The table the crew works around |
| 4 | A storyboard panel, multiplying into a filmstrip |
| 5 | The counter of the letter **F** in the wordmark |

Never cut away from it. Every transition is the rectangle *becoming* the next thing.

## Look

Near-black void. **The character is the only light source.** No purple gradients, no neon,
no glass — the site already does that, and the opening should feel like cinema, not like a
SaaS product. Think projector light in a dark room: warm key on the character, deep falloff,
a faint dust-in-the-beam haze.

**Use your own mascot as a character reference image on every cut.** Do not let the model
invent the character — lock it, or the morph breaks.

# CUT 1 — The Empty Frame (0.0–2.8s)

> *The problem, stated without words.*

### Image prompt — START frame
```
A small round cartoon character seen from behind, tiny and alone in a vast black void,
standing before an enormous blank white rectangle that towers over them like a cinema
screen. No cape yet. Shoulders low, arms at their sides — hesitant. The white rectangle
is the only light in the frame and it spills a cold pool of light onto the floor around
the character. Volumetric haze, faint dust motes drifting in the beam. Extreme wide shot,
character occupying less than a tenth of the frame height, deep negative space above.
Cinematic 2.39:1 composition inside a 16:9 frame, matte black background, high contrast,
soft film grain. Character design matched exactly to the reference image.
```

### Image prompt — END frame
```
Same composition and same character from behind, but the camera has pushed slightly closer
and the blank white rectangle now fills more of the frame. A single warm amber point of
light has appeared at the exact centre of the white rectangle — small, sharp, like the first
spark of an idea. The cold white light is beginning to warm at its centre. Same volumetric
haze and dust, same matte black void, same soft grain. Character design matched exactly to
the reference image.
```

### Video prompt
```
Extremely slow push-in toward the character and the towering blank white screen. The
character shifts their weight once, uncertain. Dust motes drift slowly through the beam of
light. At the very end of the shot a single warm amber point of light ignites in the dead
centre of the white rectangle. Nothing else moves. Locked, patient, unhurried camera. No
cuts, no text, no logos.
```

# CUT 2 — The Spark and the Cape (2.8–5.6s)

> *The moment the tool arrives. The character stops being alone.*

### Image prompt — START frame
```
Continuation: the small round cartoon character seen from behind in a black void, facing a
large white rectangle with a single warm amber point of light glowing at its centre. The
warm light is beginning to bleed outward into the cold white. The character has lifted their
head toward it. Volumetric haze, dust in the beam, matte black surround, soft film grain.
Character design matched exactly to the reference image.
```

### Image prompt — END frame
```
The same character, now turned three-quarters toward camera, wearing a flowing hero cape
that has just unfurled and is caught mid-billow, lit warm amber from the rectangle behind
them. Their posture is upright and confident, chin lifted. The rectangle behind is now
glowing warm gold rather than cold white, and the amber light rims the character's
silhouette and the edge of the cape. Dramatic rim lighting, black void surround, volumetric
haze, soft film grain. Character design matched exactly to the reference image.
```

### Video prompt
```
The amber spark at the centre of the white rectangle blooms outward, flooding the cold
white surface with warm gold light. As the light reaches the character, a hero cape unfurls
from their shoulders in one smooth confident motion and billows in the light. The character
turns toward camera and straightens up. The camera pushes in with the bloom. Warm rim light
sweeps across the character as the cape settles. Smooth, triumphant, single continuous move.
No cuts, no text.
```

# CUT 3 — One Becomes Five (5.6–8.4s)

> *The Creator's Room — our actual differentiator, in the title sequence.*

### Image prompt — START frame
```
The caped round cartoon character standing confidently facing camera, warm amber rim light,
in a black void. Directly behind them the glowing rectangle has rotated and tilted to lie
flat, becoming a luminous table surface seen in perspective. The character stands at the
head of it. Motion-blurred echoes of the character are just beginning to separate from
their silhouette on both sides, like a figure about to split. Cinematic wide shot, matte
black background, volumetric haze, soft film grain. Character design matched exactly to the
reference image.
```

### Image prompt — END frame
```
Five identical caped round cartoon characters arranged around a glowing rectangular table
in a black void, each in a different working pose and each holding a different small prop:
one gesturing with an open hand as if directing, one writing in a notebook, one framing a
shot with a small viewfinder, one working with a strip of film, one simply watching with
arms folded. Each character is lit by the warm glow rising from the table beneath them,
casting light upward onto their faces. Symmetrical wide composition, matte black surround,
volumetric haze, soft film grain. All five characters matched exactly to the reference
image, identical to one another.
```

### Video prompt
```
The single caped character multiplies: four duplicates peel outward from the original in a
smooth fanning motion and settle into position around the glowing table, each falling
naturally into a different working pose — directing, writing, framing, cutting film,
watching. Their capes settle in sequence, one after another, not all at once. The camera
cranes up and back to reveal the full circle of five. Warm light rises from the table onto
their faces. Confident, choreographed, continuous. No cuts, no text.
```

# CUT 4 — The Frame Fills (8.4–11.2s)

> *The work happening. The empty frame from Cut 1 finally has something in it.*

### Image prompt — START frame
```
Overhead view looking straight down at a glowing rectangular table in a black void, with
five identical tiny caped cartoon characters working around its edge. The table surface is
beginning to show a faint image forming within it, like a photograph developing. Warm
amber light, matte black surround, volumetric haze, soft film grain. Characters matched
exactly to the reference image.
```

### Image prompt — END frame
```
Overhead view of a black void filled with a flowing ribbon of luminous rectangular
storyboard panels, arranged like a strip of film curving through space, each panel glowing
warm amber and containing a simple graphic shape suggesting a different film shot. The five
tiny caped characters are dotted along the ribbon, small against it. The ribbon sweeps from
the lower left toward the upper right and recedes into darkness. Cinematic overhead
composition, matte black surround, volumetric haze, soft film grain. Characters matched
exactly to the reference image.
```

### Video prompt
```
An image develops inside the glowing table like a photograph in a tray. The single panel
then divides and multiplies — one rectangle becomes many, unfurling into a long ribbon of
storyboard panels that sweeps out through the black void like a strip of film catching the
light. The five tiny caped characters ride along the ribbon as it forms. The camera lifts
and banks smoothly to follow the ribbon as it curves away into darkness. Fluid, weightless,
continuous. No cuts, no text.
```

# CUT 5 — The Name (11.2–14.0s)

> *The film resolves into the brand. The rectangle finds its final form.*

### Image prompt — START frame
```
A long ribbon of glowing amber rectangular film panels sweeping through a black void,
curving toward camera and beginning to straighten out and align into a horizontal row.
The panels are drawing closer together. Motion blur on the outer panels. Matte black
surround, volumetric haze, soft film grain.
```

### Image prompt — END frame
```
The wordmark FIRST FRAME set in a clean confident modern typeface, glowing warm amber and
centred in a pure black field. The counter of the letter F is a perfect small rectangle
that reads as a film frame. A single tiny caped cartoon character stands on the baseline
beside the final letter, small and proud, silhouetted against the glow. Vast negative
space, elegant editorial composition, matte black background, subtle volumetric haze,
soft film grain. Character matched exactly to the reference image.
```

### Video prompt
```
The ribbon of glowing film panels rushes toward camera, straightens, and collapses inward —
the panels compressing and locking together to form the letterforms of the wordmark
FIRST FRAME, which settles and holds, glowing warm amber in a black field. As the letters
lock into place a single tiny caped character walks in from the edge and stops on the
baseline beside the last letter. The haze settles. Everything comes to rest. Hold on the
final composition. Elegant, decisive, continuous. No additional text.
```

## Music

```
A 14-second cinematic logo sting. Begins almost silent — a single sustained low tone and
the faint hum of a projector. At 3 seconds a soft warm piano note enters, like a light
switching on. From 5 seconds a gentle pulse builds underneath, adding warm strings and a
soft rhythmic click reminiscent of a film transport. At 11 seconds it opens into one full,
bright, hopeful chord that resolves and decays into silence. Modern, clean, optimistic,
restrained — the sound of a title card for a technology company that loves cinema.
No drums until the final third. No vocals. No heavy bass.
```

## Production notes

- **Lock the character.** Feed your mascot as a reference/consistency image on *every*
  generation. Without it the character drifts and the morph reads as five unrelated clips.
- **Generate the END frame of each cut as the START frame of the next.** That is what makes
  the sequence seamless — cut N's end image *is* cut N+1's start image.
- **Colour discipline.** Cut 1 is cold white. Everything after is warm amber. That single
  temperature shift carries the whole emotional arc: alone → supported.
- **Deliverables** for `assets/hero/`:
  - `first-frame-hero.webm` and `.mp4` — 16:9 master
  - `first-frame-hero-1x1.webm` — square crop for mobile
  - `first-frame-hero.jpg` — the Cut 5 final composition, as the poster/fallback
- Keep the last frame graded to match the site background so the video can rest under the
  hero headline without a visible seam.
