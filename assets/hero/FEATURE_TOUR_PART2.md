Full arc across both parts:

## Same rules as Part 1 — do not break them

- **Bridge images are generated once and used twice** — as the END of one cut and the START
  of the next. Cut N is generated with `start = B(n-1)`, `end = B(n)`.
- **Overlap 8–10 frames at every junction** and cross-dissolve. Identical stills make the
  join invisible.
- **The character never stops moving at a bridge.** Every bridge image is mid-action.
- **No readable text in any generation.** Abstract UI geometry only — panels, bars, chips,
  swatches, waveforms. Real UI is composited on top afterwards.
- **Feed the mascot as a character reference on every single generation.**

### Motion continuity

| Bridge | Character is… | Direction | Screen size | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B5** *(from Part 1)* | mid-jump, arms spread | → forward, airborne | ~12% | pulling back |
| **B6** | reaching out, grabbing | → forward, being pulled | ~20% | tracking in |
| **B7** | riding forward, crouched | → right, fast | ~18% | tracking right |
| **B8** | landing, arms opening | ↓ then settling | ~15% | craning down |
| **B9** | standing still, small | — at rest | ~8% | locked off |

**B9 is the only place the character is allowed to stop.** That is the ending.

# THE CUTS

## CUT 6 — The Tools (0.0–4.5s) · *AI Tools* · **B5 → B6**

> *Choosing your equipment. The wall of options resolves into one right answer.*

```
The character sails forward through the air and the space fills with dozens of glowing
rectangular modules of light drifting at different depths, each a different colour, each
carrying small abstract bars and chips. The character slows and hovers, turning to scan
them. The modules sort themselves in a smooth wave, sliding and reordering around the
character as if being filtered, until three modules rise and brighten above the rest. The
character reaches out and closes a hand around the brightest one, which flares and yanks
them forward, cape streaming. The camera tracks in with the pull. One continuous move, no
cuts, no readable text.
```

**Composite on top afterwards:** tool names, price chips, spec rows, and the *AI Tools* label.

## CUT 7 — The Edit (4.5–9.0s) · *Editing & assembly* · **B6 → B7**

> *Everything made so far becomes one thing.*

```
The glowing module in the character's hand stretches and unrolls into a long horizontal band
of rectangular clips running away to the right through near-black space, with a luminous
waveform forming beneath it. The character lands on the band and crouches, riding it forward
at speed. As they pass, clips slide into place, shuffle order, and snap tight against one
another; a few are trimmed shorter with a quick swipe of the character's hand. Behind them
the assembled clips compress together into a single dense bright block of light that keeps
pace. The camera tracks alongside in a fast smooth side-on move. Continuous, rhythmic, no
cuts, no readable text.
```

**Composite on top afterwards:** timeline ruler, clip labels, waveform detail, the *Edit* label.

## CUT 8 — The Room (9.0–14.0s) · *Community, kids, showcase* · **B7 → B8**

> *The warmest cut in the film. This is who it is for.*

```
The band of clips ahead of the character compresses into a single bright block, which lifts
and unfolds into a large warm glowing screen hanging in a dark open space. The camera cranes
down and back to reveal a crowd gathering in front of it — caped characters of many
different sizes drifting in from all sides, small child-sized ones settling cross-legged at
the front, taller ones standing behind, some turning toward each other in pairs. Soft rounded
speech-bubble shapes of light rise between them and drift gently upward, a few passing back
and forth between characters as though answering one another. The original character lands
among them with arms opening outward, and several nearby characters turn and lift a hand in
greeting. Everyone is rim-lit warm from the screen. The motion decelerates into warmth.
Continuous, no cuts, no readable text.
```

**Composite on top afterwards:** the reaction chips — *Story · Light & Colour · Sound ·
Editing* — floating inside the speech bubbles, and the *Showcase* label.

## CUT 9 — Everything Becomes the Name (14.0–18.0s) · **B8 → B9**

> *The gathering resolves into the brand.*

```
The floating speech bubbles, the glowing screen, and the panels from the whole journey lift
off and stream inward through the black space, compressing and locking together to form the
letterforms of the wordmark FIRST FRAME, which settles and holds, glowing warm amber in a
black field. As the letters lock into place the crowd of small caped characters, tall and
child-sized together, walks in and comes to rest in a row along the baseline beneath the
wordmark, seen from behind. The haze settles. Everything comes to rest and holds.
Elegant, decisive, continuous. No additional text.
```

# SOUND — continuing the cute palette

Same rules as Part 1: soft mallet percussion, rounded synth pops, tiny bells, gentle
whooshes. Nothing metallic, nothing harsh. **Every cue lands on the character's body.**

| Time | Action | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.6s | modules fill the space | soft airy bloom, many small flutters |
| 1.4–2.4s | modules sort and filter | **a quick cascade of light wooden clicks**, sliding |
| 2.6s | three modules rise | three ascending glockenspiel notes |
| **3.4s** | **the grab** | **satisfying rounded "clunk-pop" + bright flare shimmer** |
| 3.8s | yanked forward | rising whoosh |
| 5.0s | module unrolls into a timeline | soft unfurling rasp, warm and papery |
| 5.4s | lands on the band | cushioned thump |
| 5.4–8.4s | riding the timeline | steady rhythmic soft ticks, locked to the music |
| 6.2s, 7.0s | clips snap into place | **two rounded snap-pops**, pitched a step apart |
| 7.6s | a clip is trimmed | quick light "snip", soft not sharp |
| 8.4s | clips compress into a block | descending compress-whoosh landing on a soft thud |
| 9.4s | the screen unfolds | warm expanding swell |
| 10.0–12.0s | the crowd gathers | **soft rounded footsteps arriving from all sides**, layered and gentle |
